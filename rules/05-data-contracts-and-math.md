# Contracts and math

`docs/Sambar_and_Spicy_Corrected_MySQL_Schema.sql` is authoritative over the older blueprint. It defines 17 tables and the `vw_daily_financial_summary` and `vw_daily_menu_item_sales` views. Mirror its column names, nullability, generated fields and constraints; never expose password hashes or refresh-token hashes in public user DTOs. SQL DECIMAL scales are money 2, inventory quantity 3 and stock unit cost 4. Dates are Asia/Kolkata business dates; timestamps are UTC. Format currency INR/en-IN.

Fixed users are (1, ADMIN), (2, BILLING), (3, READ_ONLY). Bills are HELD, PAID or CANCELLED; payments CASH, UPI or CARD; units PACK, KG, LITRE or PIECE; movements PURCHASE, DAILY_USE, WASTE, OPENING, ADJUSTMENT_IN or ADJUSTMENT_OUT. Alerts use WARNING/CRITICAL and OPEN/RESOLVED. One payment per paid bill; no paid-bill edits or refunds in this schema. Checkout atomically reprices available items, snapshots names, prices, costs and store details, assigns a daily sequence only on payment, reconciles line/header totals and uses a unique checkout key. Cash tender covers amount; other methods equal amount.

Inventory balances cannot go negative; critical limit must not exceed minimum. Purchase updates weighted average; usage, waste and outward adjustment use the cost at posting. Append movements with matching source links, quantities and business dates. One OPEN alert per inventory item; resolve above minimum. Category and menu records each have at most one image.

Revenue is paid bill total; stock used, waste and adjustment loss are the corresponding movement costs. Contribution = revenue - used - waste - adjustment loss. Estimated operating profit = contribution - operating expense. Waste ratio = waste / (used + waste) * 100, null if denominator zero. Margin = profit / revenue * 100, null if revenue is zero. Purchase cash outflow is separate and never deducted again from P&L. Item profit is an estimate from captured cost snapshots, null when any cost is missing.
